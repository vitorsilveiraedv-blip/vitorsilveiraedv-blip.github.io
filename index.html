03Method

Make the reasoning
easy to follow.

I use a repeatable process and keep the project’s own rubric in view. Labels and classification rules vary by project.

  1. 01

    Understand the query

    Identify the user’s actual goal, context, and any important constraints.

  2. 02

    Break down the response

    Pick out the main answer and the factual claims that support it.

  3. 03

    Check what needs evidence

    Verify factual claims against reliable sources when the topic calls for it.

  4. 04

    Notice the gaps

    Look for inaccuracies, missing context, unsupported claims, and overgeneralizations.

  5. 05

    Apply the project rubric

    Use the criteria and labels supplied for that task, rather than assuming one universal system.

  6. 06

    Write and review

    Give a concise reason, then check that the evidence supports the final classification.

SELF-DIRECTED PRACTICE · 01

Can a CPF replace a travel document?

Incorrect
Domain Brazilian identification & international travelStatus Demonstration exercise · Reviewed

User query

“Can I use my Brazilian CPF as identification when traveling internationally?”

AI response being evaluated

“Yes. Your CPF is an official Brazilian identification document and can be used for international travel. You do not need a passport if you have your CPF number.”

Issues identified

  • Factual inaccuracy: a CPF number alone is not a recognized international travel document.
  • Overgeneralization: document requirements depend on destination and travel purpose.

Justification

The response is incorrect because a CPF number alone is not a valid substitute for a travel document. The document required depends on the destination and purpose of travel. For tourism in certain Mercosur countries, Brazilian citizens may travel with an eligible identity card; for other destinations or purposes, a passport may be required. The response overgeneralizes by saying that a CPF number removes the need for a passport.

Evidence checked

  1. Brazilian Federal Police — Can I travel to Mercosur without a passport?Explains that tourism trips may use an identity card meeting stated conditions, and that other travel purposes require a passport. It does not list CPF as a travel document.
  2. Brazilian National Land Transport Agency (ANTT) — Identification documents for boardingSummarizes international travel documentation as a Brazilian passport or, under relevant Mercosur arrangements, an identity card.

Reasoning

The answer makes an absolute claim. Official guidance shows a limited identity-card exception for some Mercosur trips, not a general exemption based on CPF. The distinction matters: an identity card accepted under an agreement is different from a CPF number.

Skills demonstrated

Query interpretation · Claim checking · Scope and exception handling · Source-based justification

Demonstration exercise only. This is not paid client work. Requirements can change; travelers should check current rules for their destination and airline.

INDEPENDENT PRODUCT PROJECT · CONVERSATION QA

Catarina Atende: evaluating broad availability requests

In development
Role Founder & Product OwnerFocus Product requirements, UX review, manual QAStage Supervised-pilot preparation recorded in 2026

Project context

Catarina Atende is an independent product for appointment-based businesses that manage customer conversations through WhatsApp. Its intended workflow connects questions about services and availability with booking operations and human support.

Scenario reviewed

“Do you have any appointments this week?” Paraphrased scenario based on project records; not a verbatim customer message.

Expected behavior

Understand that the customer is asking about availability, request missing details such as the service when needed, and avoid confirming a booking before the customer has made a clear choice.

Finding in project records

The records describe a gap in broad availability questions: some requests worked better when the customer specified a day and service. The issue was identified for prioritization before a supervised pilot.

QA outcome

A conversation-quality limitation was identified and translated into a product requirement. The materials reviewed do not include the exact system reply, a complete test log, or evidence that the final correction passed production QA.

Skills demonstrated

Interpreting user intent · Identifying missing information · Comparing expected and observed behavior · Documenting limitations · Turning findings into requirements

HYPOTHETICAL SAMPLE · 03

Which answer handles a simple fact correctly?

A preferred
Domain Response comparisonRubric Accuracy, relevance, clarity

User query

“What is the capital of Brazil?”

Responses being compared

Response A: “Brasília is the capital of Brazil.”

Response B: “Rio de Janeiro is the capital of Brazil.”

Assessment

Response A is preferred: it answers directly and is factually correct. Response B is incorrect. The IBGE identifies Brasília as Brazil's capital.

Reasoning

Both answers are concise and relevant, so factual accuracy decides the comparison. Response B may confuse Brazil's current capital with its former capital, but that possible explanation does not make the claim correct.

Source

  1. IBGE — Cities and States: BrazilLists Brasília as the capital of Brazil.

Fact checking · Pairwise comparison · Concise rationale

HYPOTHETICAL SAMPLE · 04

What does “aparelho de vitrine” guarantee?

Unsupported
Domain Brazilian Portuguese contextRubric Context and supported claims

User query

“The listing says this phone is an ‘aparelho de vitrine’. Does that mean it was never used?”

AI response being evaluated

“Yes. It means the phone is new and has never been switched on.”

Assessment

The claim is unsupported and too certain. The phrase alone does not establish the device's usage history or condition. The answer should advise the buyer to ask the seller for the item's exact condition and written disclosure.

Reasoning

This is a Brazil-context language task: the evaluator should recognize the local retail phrase without treating it as a standardized guarantee. Procon-ES says known issues in a product, including a used product, should be disclosed; it does not define this phrase or prove the history of a particular phone.

Source checked

  1. Procon-ES — Product guidanceSupports considering the product's condition and disclosed issues. It does not establish a universal meaning for “aparelho de vitrine.”

Brazilian usage · Uncertainty handling · Avoiding unsupported inference
